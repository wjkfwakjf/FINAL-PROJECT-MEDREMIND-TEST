01 · INTRODUCTION

Medication care, made simple.

Missed medicine is one of the most common and most preventable causes of poor treatment results. Patients forget doses, clinics keep scattered paper records, and doctors cannot easily see whether a prescription is being followed.

MedRemind Care brings these pieces together. A desktop app lets the care team register patients, assign medicines with reminder times and record each dose as taken or missed. This website lets clients browse a medicine library and send consultation questions that the admin answers.

02 · STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM

What goes wrong today.

Clinics, caregivers and patients who manage medicine by hand face these problems.

PROBLEM 01

Forgotten doses

Patients forget or confuse schedules, leading to missed or doubled doses.

PROBLEM 02

Scattered records

Patient, medicine and appointment details live on paper or in separate files, so they are easy to lose.

PROBLEM 03

No dose history

Without a record of taken and missed doses, adherence cannot be measured or reviewed.

PROBLEM 04

Unanswered questions

Clients have no convenient way to ask about a medicine before seeing a professional.

PROBLEM 05

Disconnected visits

Appointment dates and doctors are not linked to each patient's medicine plan.

THE QUESTION

How can one system help?

How can a simple, central system improve medication adherence and make care records easier to manage?

03 · OBJECTIVES

What the project sets out to do.

◎
GENERAL OBJECTIVE

To develop MedRemind Care, a computerised system that manages patient records, medicine schedules, reminders, appointments and client consultations in one place, with the aim of improving medication adherence and record keeping.

Specific objectives

  1. 01

    Register and manage patient information such as name, age and contact.

  2. 02

    Add, update and delete medicines per patient, using a built-in catalog of brands, uses and strengths.

  3. 03

    Show timed reminders and let staff mark each dose as Taken or Missed.

  4. 04

    Keep a permanent medication history, even if a medicine is later deleted.

  5. 05

    Record doctor appointments linked to each patient.

  6. 06

    Provide an admin dashboard with totals and consultation activity.

  7. 07

    Offer a secure client website with sign-up, login, a medicine library and consultations.

  8. 08

    Separate administrator and client roles so each user sees only what they should.

04 · SCOPE OF THE STUDY

What the system covers.

IN SCOPE

Included

  • Patient registration and records
  • Medicine management with a 13-medicine catalog
  • Timed reminders, checked every 30 seconds
  • Taken and missed dose history
  • Appointment and doctor records
  • Client website with consultations and admin replies
  • Role-based sign-in with password reset by admin
OUT OF SCOPE

Not included

  • Diagnosis or prescribing
  • SMS, email or mobile push reminders
  • Drug-interaction and allergy checks
  • Large hospital networks
  • Online payments or pharmacy delivery
05 · LIMITATIONS OF THE STUDY

Known limits.

Please note

Reminders appear only while the desktop app is open. The system suits a single clinic or small team. The medicine library is general information and never replaces a doctor or pharmacist; users must follow their prescription and package label. The built-in Flask server is for development, so public use needs HTTPS and a production server.

11 · CONCLUSION

A calmer way to manage medicine.

MedRemind Care shows that a simple, well-organised system can make medication management more reliable. By joining patient records, reminders, dose tracking, appointments and client consultations, it lowers the chance of missed medicine and gives caregivers and doctors the information they need. Future work could add SMS or email reminders, a mobile app, interaction warnings, charts and exportable reports.

13 · DATABASE

The data model.

One shared database serves both the desktop app and this website.

patients 1 → many medicinespatients 1 → many appointments medicines 1 → many medication_historyusers 1 → many consultations

users

  • username (key)
  • full_name
  • role
  • password (hashed)
  • created_at

patients

  • id (key)
  • full_name
  • age
  • contact

medicines

  • id (key)
  • patient_id
  • medicine_name
  • dosage
  • reminder_time
  • status
  • notes

appointments

  • id (key)
  • patient_id
  • doctor_name
  • appointment_date

medication_history

  • id (key)
  • medicine_id
  • patient_id
  • medicine_name
  • dosage
  • status
  • dose_date
  • recorded_at

consultations

  • id (key)
  • client_username
  • medicine_name
  • question
  • status
  • admin_reply
  • created_at

Medication history copies the patient and medicine names, so records stay readable even after a medicine is deleted. Passwords are stored as hashes, never as plain text.
